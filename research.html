Research

Research Interests

Integrative comparative genomics approaches to address key biological questions

Major Projects

Haplotype-resolved reference genome assembly for autotetraploid alfalfa

Developed an integrative pipeline combining different Hi-C scaffolding tools to generate a high-quality haplotype-resolved genome for autotetraploid alfalfa, uncovering substantial structural and sequence divergence among its four haplotypes, with this pattern conserved across other autotetraploid species.

Identification and evolution of self-incompatibility locus in alfalfa

Leveraged an haplotype-resolved alfalfa genome to devise a new strategy MOASI (https://github.com/kedduck/MOASI) for S-locus identification, focused in depth on a candidate S-locus, and demonstrated through comparative and phylogenetic analyses that the stepwise evolutionary assembly of the S-locus.

Evolutionary analyses of self-incompatibility loci in wild race

Using Oryza longistaminata as a study system, I reconstructed S and Z locus genes from resequencing data via de novo genome assemblies, enabling haplotype resolution in highly divergent genomic regions. Through comparative genomics across Oryza, I characterized the evolutionary history of S and Z genes and showed that patterns of mutation accumulation are consistent with species phylogeny.
